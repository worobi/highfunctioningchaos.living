About HFC

Still figuring it out.

High Functioning Chaos comes from our life: marriage, parenting, neurodivergence, work, school, and the things that don’t fit neatly around any of it.

Brandon

Army combat veteran. AuDHD. Usually the one asking why a process is broken and then trying to rebuild it. Dry humor comes with the territory.

Monica

Brings warmth, reflection, and her own perspective on the life we share. We don’t always see the same moment the same way. That matters.

Why we share it.

We can’t undo our mistakes. We can be honest about them, own our part, and share the tools we wished we had sooner. If that helps someone else face a similar day with another option, this has a point.

Family and trusted voices contribute when it makes sense. Some parts of life stay private.

From experience.

We’re not therapists or mental health professionals. We’re living this too, making mistakes and figuring things out as we go. We share what we’ve learned in the hope that you won’t have to learn every lesson the hard way like we did—and that you’ll have a few tools in your toolbox when you need them.
