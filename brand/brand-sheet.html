The Chaos Mark

Three bars. One out of line.

The top and bottom bars align — structured, contained, holding it together. The middle bar is shorter and shifted right in crimson. It doesn't break the system. It just doesn't cooperate with it. That's the whole brand in one small mark. Not broken. Just slightly, defiantly off.

Use the mark at any size. Keep the proportions: middle bar is roughly 70% the width of the outer bars, shifted right so it has more margin on the left than the right.

Voice & Tone
Do
  • Sound like two real people talking
  • Use dark humor when it carries truth
  • Let discomfort breathe — don't rush to fix it
  • Be blunt and warm in the same breath
  • Write like the audience already gets it
  • PG-13 to R language when it fits naturally
  • Follow the arc: Chaos → Breakdown → Reflection → Takeaway
Don't
  • Therapy-speak or clinical jargon
  • Toxic positivity or "healing journey" language
  • Generic self-help advice
  • Influencer-style inspiration
  • Polished PR voice
  • Overexplaining — let readers arrive on their own
  • Humor that hides from the actual feeling
